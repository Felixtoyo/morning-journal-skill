# Journal format

Use this as an adaptable template, not a mandatory interview script. Use the user's language. The entry date is the user's local journal date, not the server date.

```text
# Morning Journal — YYYY-MM-DD

## Health observations (optional)
- Sleep: [user-reported duration/quality or authorized source; date]
- Weight: [value and unit, measurement time if known]
- Other observations: [only supplied information]

## How I feel this morning
[Subjective energy, mood, or physical sensations in the user's words.]

## On my mind
[Thoughts, concerns, or reflections.]

## Something I appreciate
[A specific observation or gratitude, if shared.]

## Today's intention
[One realistic focus or next step chosen by the user.]
```

Do not fill bracketed examples into real entries. Omit optional sections without content; if a required destination field lacks a value, resolve it before saving. Do not turn missing data into zero, normal, or a guessed score. If sleep spans midnight, retain the reported interval rather than silently changing its date. Keep conflicting measurements separate with their sources or ask which to use.
